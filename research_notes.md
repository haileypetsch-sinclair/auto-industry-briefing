## TREND 1: EV Market — Global Surge, US Slowdown
### Key Data Points
- **Global EV sales**: ~21 million EVs sold worldwide in 2025 (10x growth in 6 years); EVs now >25% of global new car sales in 2026
- **UK**: BEV registrations hit 29.8% share in June 2026 (64,440 units), up 38% YoY; Tesla +42%, BYD +9%
- **US**: EV market share declined 3.4 percentage points in Q1 2026 vs Q1 2025; 39% decrease in EV volume (~148,000 vehicles) — largely due to loss of IRA tax credits
- **US EV share**: Was ~10% in Q3 2025, dropped to 5.4% in Q1 2026
- **Canada**: EV sales share dropped from 14.7% (2024) to 9.5% (2025)
- **EV Motor Market**: Valued at $22.1B in 2025, projected to reach $120B by 2036 (CAGR 16%)
- **EV depreciation**: EV values dropped sharply in 2025, outpacing ICE vehicle depreciation
- **Tesla**: Q2 2026 EV sales grew for second consecutive quarter after two years of declines; launched Model Y L (3-row, 6-seat, $63,630 starting price, 325 miles range)
- **BYD**: Outsold Tesla across Europe in multiple months in 2026; 40,597 deliveries in June (NIO), +62.9% YoY

### Key Data Points
- **SDV Market Size**: $447.55B in 2026 → $1,707.36B by 2035 (CAGR 16%)
- **North America SDV**: $38.76B (2024) → $254.42B by 2030 (CAGR 36.8%)
- **Europe SDV**: $21.16B (2024) → $287.24B by 2030 (CAGR 54.4%) — fastest growing region

- **Key players**: Tesla (Star Player), Rivian (Emerging Leader), NVIDIA (Progressive Player), Amazon (Responsive)
- **VLA Models**: Vision-Language-Action models represent a generational leap — cars process sensor data and make real-time decisions about steering, braking, navigation
- **AMD + Turing**: AMD AI accelerators integrated into autonomous vehicle systems by Turing Inc.
- **Turing (Japan)**: Raised ¥27.89B for autonomous driving
- **Automotive Data Monetization**: $3.78B in 2026 → $14.62B by 2034 (CAGR 18.4%); 400M+ connected vehicles by 2026
- **Cybersecurity**: Growing challenge — UNECE WP.29 R155 and ISO SAE 21434 compliance required

- **USMCA Review**: July 1, 2026 USMCA review — US demanding changes that could force Canada/Mexico to surrender trade advantages
- **Reshoring**: Survey shows companies accelerating US manufacturing reshoring due to tariff uncertainty
- **Industry impact**: Tight inventory after strong holiday sales; some OEMs weighing US production shifts
- **Chinese Auto**: China produced/sold 10M+ units in first 4 months of 2025 alone; structural transformation driven by tech disruption and geopolitics
- **Honda CR-V**: Became America's #1 bestselling vehicle in H1 2026 (226,114 units) — capitalizing on Toyota RAV4 and Ford F-150 supply shortages due to model changeovers

## TREND 5: Consumer Behavior & Market Dynamics
### Key Data Points
- **Average new vehicle price**: Crossed $50,000 for first time in Sept 2025; hovering at $51,000+ MSRP in 2026; ATP ~$49,220 in May 2026
- **Monthly payments**: Up 30% over 4 years; nearly 1 in 5 new vehicles has payment over $1,000/month
- **Loan terms**: Stretching to 84 months; dealers fear negative equity spiral
- **Young buyers**: Share of new vehicle registrations among 18-34 fell from 12% (Q1 2021) to under 10% (mid-2025)
- **Older buyers**: 55+ account for nearly half of all new registrations; held largest share for 8 straight quarters
- **Long-term outlook (Bain)**: "Perfect storm" — falling birth rates, behavioral change, high prices, alternatives could reduce US sales by 2M+ units by 2040
- **Vehicle longevity**: Record 12.8 years average vehicle age on road in 2025
- **US SAAR**: June 2026 ~16.1M SAAR; June sales ~1.36M units, up 7.2% YoY
- **Aftermarket**: US aftermarket forecast to grow 5.2% in 2026, surpass $500B by 2029
- **Connected car sentiment**: S&P Global 2026 study on which features drive trim take rates and package selection
